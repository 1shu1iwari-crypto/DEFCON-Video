# DEFCON | HAM ARC × TECHNOZION — Visual Design

## Style Prompt
A cinematic cyber operations trailer: severe, tactical, signal-corrupted and legible. The visual grammar pairs brutal terminal typography with restrained luminous interfaces. The poster's distressed DEFCON wordmark sets the threat level; all logos remain in their original pixels and proportions.

## Colors
- Background: #07080B (ink-black)
- Surface: #10131A (blue-black console)
- Main text: #F2F1EE (warm signal white)
- Alert: #E51B23 (blood crimson)
- Secondary signal: #315E9B (deep electric blue)

## Typography
- Display: Oswald, weight 700-800, tight tracking
- Data / terminal: IBM Plex Mono, weight 400-600

## Motion
Fast signal glitches and hard directional moves for scene handoffs; slower, deliberate holds on both official logos and the final affiliation.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not redraw, recolor, crop, stretch, or distort either supplied logo.
- Do not invent dates, venues, fees, URLs, registration instructions, or extra prizes.
- Do not fill terminal panels with random or fake command output.
- Do not make small interface text carry essential event information.
- Do not use cyan gradients, generic hacker stock imagery, or excessive HUD decoration.
